Oliveros worked with Dave Olean, a ham radio operator who participated in one of the first two-way Earth–Moon–Earth (EME) communications; "moonbounce" was first developed through war efforts to communicate and spy across large distances before communication satellites — [source](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.7208/chicago/9780226823393-005/html)
